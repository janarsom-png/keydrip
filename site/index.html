    How the heist works

    1. The token was launched on pump.fun with 100% of creator fees locked to the vault wallet through pump.fun fee sharing. Every trade adds to the pot.
    2. The wallet's 32-byte seed is shown above in base58. Sealed glyphs are shown as •.
    3. Each step reveals one more glyph — when the market cap holds above a milestone (checked on two independent price feeds for an hour) or when its time-lock deadline passes, whichever comes first.
    4. Reconstruct the seed, derive the keypair and check it matches the wallet address. Then you control the wallet and everything in it.
    5. Fees waiting in pump.fun's vault are pushed to the wallet by a permissionless "distribute" call that anyone can trigger. Future fees keep flowing to the same wallet, because the split is locked.
    6. Whoever moves the funds first wins. That's the whole game.
    7. The endgame drips slowly (one glyph every ~7 days) so the cracking race has time to play out. Every glyph has a hard deadline, so the vault always opens eventually.

    Verify the vault

    At launch we published, for every hidden glyph, a salted commitment sha256("position|char|salt"). Each reveal publishes that glyph's char and salt, so you can verify it the instant it appears — the gold glyphs above with a dot underline are checked live in your browser.

    —

    Every sealed glyph is also time-locked with drand (League of Entropy). Its ciphertext is published at launch and can only be decrypted after the glyph's deadline — by anyone, even if we vanish. So the game is guaranteed to end. Milestones can only reveal a glyph earlier; they can never delay it. The wallet address is itself a commitment, because only one seed derives it. Rebuild the reveals yourself: node reveal/verify-reveals.js, or open a due glyph with no secret: node reveal/tlock-open.js.

    Milestone-early reveals are published by us (we hold the char+salt until then), so for those you trust us not to sit on a triggered milestone. The time-lock is the backstop: worst case, you wait for the deadline. Deadlines, not milestones, are what guarantees the ending.

    The fee routing is locked on-chain too (see Fee split). The dev's wallet has no power over where fees go.

    Honest caveat: the creator generated the puzzle, so the creator knows the key. The commitment proves the puzzle wasn't swapped, and the fee lock proves where fees go, but nothing can stop the creator from sweeping the vault wallet. Watch it on-chain, where every move is public.

    Rules & disclaimers

    • First to sweep wins. If several people crack the key, it's a race and the first confirmed transaction takes it. No refunds, no appeals, no do-overs.
    • Anyone holding the key can do anything the vault wallet can: move its SOL, trigger distributions, and sell any tokens it holds. Future creator fees keep arriving at the same wallet, so whoever controls it keeps collecting until someone else moves faster.
    • The fee split is locked by pump.fun's program (admin revoked). The only exception documented in pump.fun's SDK is a platform-run community takeover (CTO) by pump.fun itself.
    • Front-running bots exist. If you crack it, act fast and consider a private or priority transaction.
    • After someone wins: creator fees keep flowing to the same wallet, and its key is now public, so it becomes a sweeper race — whoever moves each new fee first keeps it. The split is locked and can't be repointed. Treat a win as "you get what's there now, and whatever you can grab later".
